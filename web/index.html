A share of NVIDIA stops trading at 4pm. A token that tracks it does not, so every close, halt and overnight gap hands free money to whoever trades the stale pool first. Here a Reactive contract watches the exchange and switches pools on Base and Unichain into the right mode within about two seconds. Each chain has two identical pools: one protected, one twin that ignores market hours.

  1. 1 Play the exchange: halt, close, or move the price.
  2. 2 Watch the stopwatch as both chains switch.
  3. 3 Play the villain: raid either pool and compare the loot.
